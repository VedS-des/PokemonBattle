# 6. Battle Rules

* Player selects exactly 3 Pokémon from the 6 available Pokémon.
* Each Pokémon has exactly 4 moves.
* Each move has a fixed type, power, and accuracy.
* Battle is turn-based.
* Speed determines which Pokémon attacks first.
* Damage is calculated by the battle engine.
* Type effectiveness is applied.
* HP cannot go below 0.
* A Pokémon at 0 HP is considered fainted.
* When an enemy Pokémon faints, the next enemy Pokémon enters automatically.
* When the player's Pokémon faints, the player chooses another available Pokémon.
* Battle ends when all 3 Pokémon on one side have fainted.

# 7. Special Move

### Rest

Only Snorlax has Rest.

* Power: 0
* Accuracy: 100
* Effect: Restores 30 HP.
* Rest cannot increase HP above the Pokémon's maximum HP.
